**GlassHurricaneCandleHolder.optimized.glb** (140 KB) is the file used in the store. It is a modified copy:
textures were converted to WebP and resized to 1024 px, and unused data was removed, with
`gltf-transform optimize --compress false --texture-compress webp --texture-size 1024`.
The shape and materials are otherwise unchanged.
